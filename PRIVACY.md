# Privacy

Postbarrel scripts collects nothing.

The skill is plain text instructions that Claude reads. It has no code, no connectors, no hooks and no analytics. It does not store your answers, read your files, memory or chat history, or send anything to Fuad Laguda, Postbarrel or anyone else.

Your conversation with Claude is handled by Anthropic under Anthropic's own privacy policy. When you turn on web search, Claude's own search is used to check facts, under the same policy.

Questions: https://github.com/lagudafuadtosin/postbarrel-scripts/issues
